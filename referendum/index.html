The Multidimensional Referendum

By love the King of Powys and Gwynedd was chosen. By post, every dimension may choose again.

Item 1 · The QuestionBallot

Shall Wales henceforth be known as Cymru, an independent nation?

Item 2 · How to VotePost only

  1. Take one unused postage stamp. One stamp is one voice.
  2. Place it in an envelope. A note with your name, your dimension and your century is welcome but not required.
  3. This is a Welsh ballot. Post it from within Wales, and write a return address in Wales on the back of the envelope. Ballots posted from outside Wales will not be counted.
  4. Post it to the Office of the Referendum:
Tŷ Anfeidredd,
8 Tawe,
Ystradgynlais, Powys.
Cymru 91XR

Item 3 · The CountLive ledger

Every stamp received is a ballot. Each Welsh ballot is counted and entered into the Guild's ledger, and the tally is published here. Envelopes that arrive from outside Wales are logged but set aside, uncounted.

Item 4 · The DeclarationPending

When the ledger is closed and the stamps are counted, the Time Travellers Guild will proclaim the result in the name of everyone who voted: Wales, henceforth Cymru, an independent nation.

The Multidimensional Referendum is a creative and mythological project of the Time Travellers Guild. It is a symbolic declaration, is not run by or affiliated with the Welsh Government or the UK Government, and has no legal effect. Voting is free and voluntary, sending a stamp involves no purchase, and stamps are not returned.
