Study leaf 01 / v2

Read it. Try something small. Keep a record. Come back.

This is a study companion to a photographed page carrying the Rede of the Wicca. It explains the visible ideas in new words; it is not a transcription and not a complete account. The exercise marked Guild practice is a new study prompt, not the author's instruction. The photograph remains the found object from which this reading began.

Magic here is a religious and symbolic practice, not a demonstrated way to control events, diagnose illness or change another person's mind. You can study its structure without accepting its claims. Keep ordinary action, consent and physical safety in the picture.

Study leaf 01 / Walking the path

The Rede: a shape for conduct

The visible Rede is verse doing several jobs at once: setting a social ethic, describing ritual movement, and making a calendar of moon and wind. Its opening connects trust and affection with reciprocity. The phrase Live and let live is immediately paired with fair exchange. It is not a licence to take whatever you want.

Other visible lines ask for restraint in speech and careful listening. They describe circling, rhyme, clockwise movement with a growing moon and counterclockwise movement as it diminishes. The poem carries these instructions through images and rhythm, making them easier to remember. That is the form to notice: behaviour, ritual and seasonal observation held together rather than separated into a rulebook.

The photographed page stops during the poem. This guide does not pretend it has the whole Rede. The surrounding prose makes good manners and judgment central to the path. For study, ask what a proposed working does to the people around it, not only whether it fits a ritual formula.

Guild practice / Test an intention

Write one sentence naming what you want. Under it answer: who might be affected, whose choice might be displaced, what would count as fair exchange, and what ordinary action could pursue the same aim? Rewrite the intention so it concerns your own conduct. Make them love me becomes Help me speak honestly and accept their answer. That revision changes the ethical structure, not merely the wording.

What stays partial

No invented provenance

The page stops partway through the poem. What is missing is recorded as missing, not filled from memory or from a second source.

Seven other study leaves and a register of ten objects were once on this page. They are no longer here. Their absence is not a claim that they were wrong; it is a decision about what this page can honestly hold.

Names printed on objects identify those objects. They do not identify who owned them. Photograph dates, where visible in the supplied screenshots, are not dates of manufacture.

Handwriting stays handwriting. No uncertain word has been silently repaired, and no author, relation or story has been invented for it.
