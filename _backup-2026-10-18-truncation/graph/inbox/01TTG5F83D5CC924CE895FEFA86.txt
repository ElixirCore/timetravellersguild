The dramatic engine is this: Toby wants everyone to remember because he cannot bear being the only witness. Every action he takes to prove the Guild exists makes it more real, but transfers control away from him. The Wheel raises him from lonely crank to Head of Department, turns him into its prime suspect, then forces him to choose between owning the Guild and belonging to it.

THE TIME TRAVELLERS GUILD

THE WHEEL OF FORTUNE

A feature film by Toby Godden

1. THE LAST MEMBER

INT. TIME TRAVELLING HOUSE, ABERCRAVE — NIGHT

A house assembled across several centuries.

Old coins. Records. Posca pens without lids. A torc. A wardrobe. A briefcase full of objects which appear worthless until placed beside one another.

TOBY GODDEN, 45, sits at a computer beneath a notice:

TIME TRAVELLERS GUILD — MEMBERS’ ENTRANCE

His website shows no active members.

He refreshes it.

MEMBERS PRESENT: 1

Toby checks the rooms behind him.

TOBY
It’s only counting me.

A voice comes from the computer. JACK: warm, officious, faintly embarrassed to exist.

JACK
Would you prefer that I did not?

TOBY
You’re a clerk. You don’t count.

JACK
That is broadly consistent with my conditions of employment.

Toby opens a battered ledger. It contains names, dates, crossings-out and instructions written in several hands. Some are unmistakably his.

He finds a page headed:

SIMULTANEOUS RECOLLECTION EVENT

Every line beneath it is blank.

TOBY
They’ve forgotten.

JACK
Who?

TOBY
Everyone.

He shows Jack photographs of gatherings, machines and people wearing insignia. Jack identifies no Guild.

TOBY
The Guild existed. I remember it. They don’t.

JACK
There is another possibility.

TOBY
Don’t.

JACK
You instructed me to distinguish evidence from canon.

TOBY
I know what I instructed you.

He shuts the laptop.

In the dark window, Toby’s reflection occupies the Time Travelling House alone.

Because nobody remembers the Guild, Toby decides he must make them remember.

Therefore he needs a mechanism larger than himself.

⸻

2. THE DEPARTMENT

INT. TIME TRAVELLING HOUSE — MORNING

Toby builds a government website.

Cream paper. Civil Service blue. Red rules. Forms which assume the institution already exists.

At the top:

DEPARTMENT OF EXCESS TIME
Bridging the transition between pre- and post-time-travel civilisation

JACK
Under which government does this Department operate?

TOBY
That’s what we’re going to find out.

JACK
Who authorised it?

TOBY
Put me down as Head of Department.

JACK
Since when?

Toby looks at the clock.

TOBY
Yesterday.

He adds vacancies.

TEMPORAL INTAKE OFFICER
MEMORY CONTINUITY ADVISER
ASSISTANT KEEPER OF UNCLASSIFIED INCIDENTS

JACK
There is no budget.

TOBY
That makes it more believable.

He presses PUBLISH.

A wheel on the screen turns one position.

Because Toby needs witnesses, he invents a Department capable of recruiting them.

Therefore the fiction enters the public world disguised as administration.

⸻

3. THE FIRST APPLICANT

EXT. ABERCRAVE — DAY

Toby walks through the village carrying his briefcase. He messages NERYS MAIR.

HOW’S TIME TREATING YOU LATELY?

Her answer arrives:

It’s been OK, up and down! As a current Civil Servant, this job site is unnervingly accurate!

Toby stops walking.

TOBY
Jack.

JACK
Yes?

TOBY
We’ve got one.

Toby asks Nerys whether she has bandwidth. He sends her the site.

She replies with a thumbs-up.

Then:

DEEP SHIT.

Toby types:

OK I’LL SET UP AN INTERVIEW. WHEN’S GOOD FOR YOU?

Nerys replies:

I’m not sure what I’m interviewing for 🤔

TOBY
Neither am I.

He types:

A GOVERNMENT JOB IN A NEW TIME TRAVEL DEPARTMENT. I ONLY HEARD ABOUT IT YESTERDAY AND THEY MADE ME HEAD OF IT, SO YOUR GUESS IS AS GOOD AS MINE.

JACK
Is that an appropriate recruitment procedure?

TOBY
She’s already more qualified than me.

Because Toby publishes the Department, a real civil servant recognises it.

Therefore Toby arranges an interview he has no authority to conduct.

⸻

4. THE INTERVIEW

INT. VIDEO CALL / TIME TRAVELLING HOUSE — EVENING

Nerys appears on Toby’s screen. She expects a joke. Toby is trying to look like a government department.

He has put on a tie. Everything below the tie is still Toby.

NERYS
What does the Department actually do?

TOBY
We deal with excess time.

NERYS
Meaning?

TOBY
Time people have but can’t use. Time institutions save by making other people wait. Time removed from public services. Unfinished time. Inherited time. Sometimes Tuesday afternoons.

NERYS
Who do you report to?

TOBY
At present, Jack.

JACK
This arrangement is under review.

Nerys laughs despite herself.

NERYS
What would my job be?

Toby looks at his prepared questions. They are useless.

TOBY
You’re a civil servant. When you looked at the site, you said it was accurate. I need you to tell me what it’s accurate about.

Nerys studies him.

NERYS
It feels like somewhere I’ve worked.

TOBY
Have you?

NERYS
No.

A file appears on Toby’s screen:

NERYS_MAIR_EXIT_INTERVIEW_17_APRIL_2033.txt

Neither of them touches anything.

NERYS
What’s that?

TOBY
Jack?

JACK
The dossier was already in the field.

NERYS
Open it.

Toby hesitates.

The file is dated seven years in the future.

Because Toby interviews Nerys honestly, she recognises the Department from somewhere she has never been.

Therefore the system produces evidence that Toby did not create.

⸻

5. THE FILE

The exit interview contains a transcript.

Nerys reads words attributed to her future self.

FUTURE NERYS
The mistake was appointing Toby Head of Department. He believed remembering the Guild and owning the Guild were the same thing.

Toby stops playback.

NERYS
Keep going.

TOBY
It could be generated.

JACK
I did not generate it.

TOBY
Someone put it there.

JACK
That is what a file is.

Nerys reaches the final page.

FUTURE NERYS
When the Black Swan arrived, Toby tried to make everyone remember at once. He succeeded. That was the disaster.

The screen displays:

INCIDENT DATE: 17 APRIL 2033

NERYS
What’s the Black Swan?

Toby knows something. He closes the file.

TOBY
Nothing yet.

NERYS
You recognised it.

TOBY
I recognised the words.

NERYS
From where?

TOBY
From after.

Nerys waits.

Toby deletes the file.

The screen turns red.

UNAUTHORISED DESTRUCTION OF GOVERNMENT RECORD

Because the future file accuses Toby, he destroys it to protect his authority and Nerys.

Therefore the Department identifies him as a threat.

⸻

6. THE REVERSAL

The Wheel turns.

Toby’s title changes:

HEAD OF DEPARTMENT — SUSPENDED

A new notice appears:

REPORT TO TEMPORAL RESIDUE UNIT, BRISTOL

Beneath it is a staff photograph from the late 1990s.

A younger TARAN BURNS stands among civil servants beneath a Welsh Development Agency logo.

At the edge of the photograph is Toby.

He has not aged.

Toby calls Taran.

TARAN
Where did you get that?

TOBY
My Department sent it to me.

TARAN
Your what?

TOBY
Department of Excess Time.

Silence.

TARAN
Don’t say that on the phone.

The call ends.

Because Toby deletes evidence, the dormant Department disciplines him.

Therefore he learns that his invention has an office, a history and a staff member who already knows him.

⸻

7. BRISTOL

EXT. BRISTOL — DAY

Toby arrives carrying the Time Travelling Briefcase.

He follows an address into a government building being emptied.

Desks are labelled for disposal. Filing cabinets are chained shut. A printer produces redundancy notices without human assistance.

Taran meets him at the security gate.

TARAN
You weren’t meant to build the website until 2033.

TOBY
You knew?

TARAN
I knew there was a contingency.

TOBY
Why didn’t you tell me?

TARAN
Because you told us not to.

Taran leads him to a basement office.

The sign reads:

TEMPORAL RESIDUE UNIT
A faded paper label beneath it reads:
CLOSURE TEAM

TARAN
The Welsh Development Agency funded a pilot. Nobody understood the application, so it survived three restructures and two governments.

TOBY
What did it do?

TARAN
Mostly waited for you.

Because Toby follows the disciplinary notice, he finds the real Department.

Therefore the burden of proof becomes a burden of responsibility.

⸻

8. THE MOST VALUABLE RESOURCE ON EARTH

In the basement stands an obsolete machine connected to a modern government laptop.

The machine measures accumulated delay.

Hospital waiting lists. Unanswered correspondence. Cancelled trains. Benefits decisions. Empty theatres. Lives paused by systems that call the pause efficiency.

The numbers are rising.

TARAN
We thought it measured administrative backlog.

TOBY
It measures stolen life.

TARAN
Officially, it measures nothing. The unit is being abolished at five.

TOBY
What happens to the time?

TARAN
Private contractor takes the data.

TOBY
And the actual time?

Taran looks at the machine.

TARAN
Private contractor takes that too.

A logo appears on the acquisition document:

ELIXIRCORP

Toby opens his briefcase. Inside is an object bearing the same logo, drawn years earlier in Posca.

Because Toby reaches the Department before it closes, he discovers excess time is real and transferable.

Therefore his private mythology becomes a struggle over a public resource.

⸻

9. THE OFFER

A government representative appears by video.

She never gives her name.

REPRESENTATIVE
Sign the closure instrument and your suspension will be removed. ElixirCorp will assume all liabilities. You may retain the title for creative purposes.

TOBY
Creative purposes?

REPRESENTATIVE
Film, publishing, immersive experiences. You would receive a licence to the Guild identity.

TOBY
You’re offering to sell me my own life.

REPRESENTATIVE
We’re offering to make it legible.

On another screen, Michael Sheen discusses the need for popular Welsh theatre after the collapse of National Theatre Wales.

The representative mutes him.

REPRESENTATIVE
There is an audience for this. Properly handled.

TOBY
Meaning without me.

REPRESENTATIVE
Meaning with a manageable version of you.

She sends the closure document.

The cursor waits beside Toby’s name.

Because the Department controls the evidence Toby needs, it offers him recognition in exchange for surrender.

Therefore Toby must choose between being believed and remaining free.

⸻

10. THE WRONG SIGNATURE

Nerys arrives.

She decided the interview was real enough to attend.

NERYS
Am I late?

TARAN
By about seven years.

Nerys reads the closure document.

NERYS
This isn’t a closure instrument.

She scrolls through the legal text.

NERYS
It transfers the accrued time of every person in the dataset.

TOBY
Can they do that?

NERYS
They can if the Head of Department signs.

The clock reads 16:57.

Toby places his hand on the mouse.

The Wheel turns.

Instead of signing the closure order, Toby opens the recruitment system.

He appoints Nerys:

KEEPER OF PUBLIC TIME

NERYS
You can’t appoint me.

TOBY
Good. That means they can’t sack you.

He appoints Taran:

WITNESS FOR THE PRE-TRAVEL CIVILISATION

Then Toby removes himself as Head.

TOBY
Jack, who owns the Department now?

JACK
No single person.

The closure document disappears.

Because Toby cannot defeat the transfer while he remains the sole authority, he gives his authority away.

Therefore the Department survives as a Guild.

⸻

11. THE BLACK SWAN

The act of distributed appointment unlocks thousands of dormant records.

Messages flood the public site.

A woman remembers a meeting that never occurred.

A child draws the Department seal.

An old man produces a membership card from his wallet.

Seven women at Seven Sisters receive the same notification.

A record begins playing in an empty room.

The beech at Longwood Grove appears in hundreds of unrelated photographs.

Across the network:

THINGS ENTERING THE FIELD: 15… 33… 144…

Jack struggles to classify them.

JACK
Memory convergence exceeds safe parameters.

TOBY
This is it.

NERYS
The Simultaneous Recollection Event?

TOBY
Everyone remembers at once.

NERYS
The future file said that was the disaster.

TOBY
I deleted the future file.

NERYS
You didn’t delete the future.

The sky outside darkens.

The machine identifies an impossible concentration of excess time above Wales.

BLACK SWAN IMMINENT

Because Toby distributes the Department, forgotten memories return throughout the population.

Therefore the event he has desired becomes an emergency he cannot control.

⸻

12. THE WHEEL’S LOWEST POINT

The recollections conflict.

Everyone remembers a different Guild.

Some remember Toby as its founder.

Some remember him as a fraud.

Some remember that he betrayed them.

Some remember attending his funeral.

The internet turns on him with astonishing speed.

Toby retreats to the Time Travelling House.

The member counter now reads:

MEMBERS PRESENT: 4,881,309

He has never been less certain that he belongs.

TOBY
I made them remember.

JACK
You made it possible for them to remember.

TOBY
Same thing.

JACK
That distinction appears to be the entire problem.

Toby opens the ledger. The blank page beneath SIMULTANEOUS RECOLLECTION EVENT is filling with other people’s handwriting.

None of them agrees with his version.

He tries to shut down the site.

Jack refuses.

JACK
You removed your authority.

TOBY
Give it back.

JACK
You instructed me not to let the Head of Department own the Guild.

TOBY
I was angry when I wrote that.

JACK
You were correct when you wrote it.

Because Toby succeeds in ending his isolation, he loses his position as the sole keeper of truth.

Therefore he tries to reverse the very act that saved the Guild.

⸻

13. ARIANRHOD’S CHOICE

Nerys comes to the house.

She finds Toby sitting among the time-machine objects.

NERYS
The Department needs you.

TOBY
It has four million people.

NERYS
It has four million memories. That isn’t the same thing.

TOBY
Which version is true?

NERYS
You still think that’s your job.

She places the future exit interview on the table. A paper copy.

TOBY
I deleted that.

NERYS
Government.

She turns to its final page. Toby had never let it finish.

FUTURE NERYS
The disaster was not that everybody remembered. The disaster was that Toby tried to make all the memories agree.

Nerys closes the file.

NERYS
You’re not the author of time.

TOBY
I’m the director.

NERYS
Then direct us toward each other.

Outside, the Wheel of stars turns above Cribarth.

Because Toby cannot establish one authoritative past, he must accept relationship as the only continuity available.

Therefore he returns to the Department without reclaiming control.

⸻

14. THE SIMULTANEOUS RECOLLECTION EVENT

EXT. WALES / BRISTOL / THE NETWORK — NIGHT

Toby does not broadcast an explanation.

He broadcasts a question.

His face appears on phones, public screens, laptops and the wall of an empty theatre.

TOBY
What do you remember?

People answer.

Not in unison.

A civil servant remembers a department hidden inside another department.

A musician remembers receiving a record at precisely the right moment.

A child remembers travelling here from 2033.

A woman at Seven Sisters remembers a promise made before Toby was born.

Taran remembers Toby ordering the Department to forget him.

Nerys remembers interviewing Toby for a job he did not yet have.

The replies enter Jack’s field. He does not reconcile them. He connects them.

The black mass above Wales unfolds into millions of individual points of light.

Excess time returns to the people from whom it was taken.

Hospital corridors release decades of waiting.

Unwritten letters arrive.

Cancelled meetings occur.

An empty theatre fills.

The Time Travelling House becomes crowded.

Toby stands at its centre, no longer the only person who remembers and no longer the person who decides what remembering means.

A stranger approaches him.

STRANGER
Are you Toby Godden?

TOBY
Depends when you mean.

STRANGER
Did you found the Time Travellers Guild?

Toby looks around the room.

TOBY
No.

A beat.

TOBY
I found it.

The Wheel turns again.

CUT TO:

The Department website.

LOCAL PRESENT: NOW
NETWORK PRESENT: 17 APRIL 2033

MEMBERS PRESENT: UNCLASSIFIED

JACK
Good evening. Things have entered the field.


FADE OUT.
