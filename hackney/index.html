The Hackney Drawer

A fictional correspondence archive - Time Travellers Guild

Reader's notice

Everything in this drawer is fiction. The council officers, departments, reference numbers, email addresses and records shown here are invented. No real Hackney Council officer, unit or record is represented, and nothing here is a recovered document.

The disappearance of the milk float from Gillett Square in the early hours of 25 December 2012 is testimony, not established fact. This drawer imagines what the paperwork might have sounded like if anyone had answered for it.

To: Street Scene Enquiries <streetscene@boroughmail.example>
Date: 27 December 2012, 09:41
Subject: Removal of vehicle from Gillett Square, Christmas morning

Dear Sir or Madam,

I live above the square. At roughly four in the morning on Christmas Day I was woken by a low humming, not a motor, more like a fridge heard through a wall. I looked out. Two figures in dark overalls were winching the old milk float onto a flatbed. The float had been parked there for weeks. The flatbed had no livery I could read.

The square was quiet again by half four. I would like to know whether the council authorised this removal, and if so where the float has been taken.

Yours faithfully,
Margery Fell

Dear Mr Tooth,

Thank you, but you have answered a different question. I did not ask whether removals were scheduled. I asked whether the council authorised this one.

The float did not belong to me. It belonged, as far as anyone locally can establish, to no one and to everyone. It had been charging off a lamp post. Some of us had taken to calling it a time machine, half in jest, because of the things left in the back of it and because it never seemed to be in quite the same place two mornings running. That is only what we called it. I would simply like to know where it is.

M. Fell

Date: 21 January 2013, 16:33
Subject: RE: SS/2013/0114

Dear Ms Fell,

I can confirm that the council holds no record of authorising the removal you describe. Please note that the absence of an authorisation record is not in itself evidence that a removal occurred.

I note your description of the vehicle. This office is not able to log enquiries under informal names, and I have filed your reference to a "time machine" as a nickname only. No abandoned-vehicle log entry corresponds to the date and location you give.

Regarding the lamp post: unauthorised electrical connection to street lighting is a matter for the lighting contractor. I have no record of a report from the contractor concerning Gillett Square over the period in question.

This correspondence is now closed.

Grant Tooth
Street Scene Enquiries

Date: 22 January 2013, 08:15
Subject: Gillett Square enquiry (SS/2013/0114)

Grant,

The Fell enquiry. I have checked with the pound, with lighting, and with the markets team, since the object is described as a former milk delivery vehicle. Nobody has it. Nobody sent a flatbed. Nobody logged a job on Christmas morning, which is what you would expect, since nobody works Christmas morning.

One thing, off the record, which I would prefer stayed off the record: the night shift supervisor at the depot remembers a flatbed signing out around 23:40 on 24 December and signing back in at 05:10 on 25 December with no job sheet attached. He does not remember the driver. I have not put this in writing anywhere else and I would ask that you don't either.

The correct line remains: no record of authorisation, no record of removal, no record of the vehicle. Which is all true, as far as the records go.

P. A.
