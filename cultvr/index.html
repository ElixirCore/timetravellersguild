CULTVR · FULLDOME

Temporal Relay Booth · Mk VI, rebuilt for the dome. The booth hangs in front of you; the geocentric orrery is the sky above.

HOW TO USE IT

  • Projection mode fills the screen with the dome image on black. Press Esc to return. Use it on the dome's media server through a browser, or save a PNG for a still.
  • Domemaster: square, 180° equidistant fisheye. Zenith at the centre, front of the room at the bottom edge.
  • Cube cross: six square faces in a 4×3 cross for software that wants a cube map.
  • Look around: drag to turn your head. This is a normal perspective view, not the dome image.
  • Keys: Space pauses, M changes view, P is projection mode.

WHAT IS NOT KNOWN

  • CULTVR's required input format, resolution and frame rate have not been confirmed with the venue. This uses the common domemaster layout. Confirm before delivering anything.
  • The Save PNG button gives stills. It does not make video.
  • Sizes above what your graphics card supports will fail with a message.
  • The scene shares assets with /box: the booth, stars and orrery come from the same files.
