A residency proposal · an empty unit · Swansea city centre

The Time Travellers Guild is an artistic community founded by Toby Godden. Its worlds, instruments and records already run live in the browser, and it convenes in person when a door opens.

The proposal is simple: you give us the keys to an empty unit, we wizard up a Time Travellers Guild. A working residency of installation, live instruments, events and workshops, turning a dead space into somewhere people come in, stay, and bring others.

The Guild is looking for a rent-free space, with responsibility for insurance, service charges, rates and utilities to be agreed before any commitment is made. What the unit gets back is footfall and a reason to be talked about.
