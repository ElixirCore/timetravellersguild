What Toby is making

draft wording The paragraphs below are a starting draft for Toby to edit, not final copy.

I'm building The Time Machine in my living room in the Upper Swansea Valley. Its main mass is a CUBE amplifier. Around it I will construct the machine, test its parts, and document what happens as it takes shape.

This grows out of Ffilm.org, my film and interactive practice, and the Time Travellers Guild: a world of people, places, records and encounters. Jack, the Guild's punchcard Clerk, is an independent character I created through Godden Interactive. His place in this work connects the machine to the Guild's archive and stories.

I'm asking you to help fund the making and its documentation. You'll see the build develop: the parts, the experiments, the failures, the changes and the finished work.

What would be delivered is the built artwork and its documentation. It does not promise physical travel through time.

The Guild and Jack

The Time Travellers Guild is an artwork, storyworld and participatory institution with websites, objects, characters and records. Its archive keeps provenance: a claim can stay pending and does not become canon on its own. A human keeper decides what becomes canon.

Jack is the Clerk of the Guild, a punchcard character who receives statements, files evidence and proposes connections.

Jack - an independent IP character created and designed by Toby Godden / Godden Interactive. The Clerk of the Time Travellers Guild.

Jack's role in this edition is TBD. Language models and voice synthesis are services behind Jack's voice. They are not his author.

Visit the Guild · Talk to Jack

Two existing pieces, seen live

/box - CHRONOS. A 3D red telephone kiosk, titled "CHRONOS, Temporal Relay Booth, Mk VI". You orbit and zoom it, and its controls are Exch (exchanges), Doors, Lamp, View, Reset and a main Dial button. In a live test on 22 October 2026, pressing Dial connected the booth to an exchange ("2026 - Pluto, Kuiper Belt"), showed a small pixel Jack panel, and turned the button into Hang Up. Jack's spoken answers were not tested for this page. It is a web page. It is not the CUBE amplifier, and the two are not the same thing.

ffilm.org/bws - T6 Gimbal Rig. A browser simulation of a red single-decker bus on a three-axis gimbal, driven by Mike "Bane" Worgan. Its panels show speed, gear, pitch, roll, G-force, a map and a sign. It is a simulation. No physical gimbal rig has been built, and none is proposed here unless Toby adds one and budgets it.

Which of these belong inside the funded scope is TBD.
