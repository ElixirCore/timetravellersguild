How it is built

Two layers, one ledger,
and a ceiling

The old voice and the plain record

The company has two layers and keeps them apart. The first is the lore: its history, its name, the craft that gave rise to it. The second is the law: a registered company with accounts, tax and directors.

The name is old. The registration is new. The IP is real.

The Ceiling

ElixirCorp's financial headroom is capped by its own constitution at one pound less than one billion, because wealth without limit warps the people who hold it. Anything beyond the line passes to a commons fund.
