Abstract

FEED∞ is a four-dimensional information-navigation instrument in which the physical manner of observing one media object contributes to the selection and presentation of subsequent media.

Machine interpretation of visual material is combined with non-identifying encounter telemetry including dwell, velocity, acceleration, recurrence, gesture and available device-field measurements.

These measurements form a continuously changing FIELD STATE.

MEDIA + MACHINE INTERPRETATION + ENCOUNTER ↓ FIELD STATE ↓ NEXT STATE

Filed Mechanism

The FIELD STATE controls semantic navigation, attention residue, temporal and geographical projection, Chronometer instrumentation and GPU field-surface deformation.

Machine-generated annotations may operate as SEMANTIC WORMHOLES, altering the topology from which subsequent material is selected.

The apparently linear infinite feed is therefore a projection of a multidimensional information field.
