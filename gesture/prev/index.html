Learn to speak hand.

Twelve hand shapes play Procrastinatrix Draw and Morph. This course teaches them one at a time, watching your hands through the camera and showing you, finger by finger, what it sees.

The camera image stays on your device. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
