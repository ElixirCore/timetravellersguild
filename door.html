It does not predict.
It computes.

Instrument №13 takes the true positions of the bodies overhead at any moment named — behind us or ahead — and puts them where they actually are. There is nothing mystical in the arithmetic. The solar system is a clock, and clocks run both ways if you know their gearing.

FRAGMENT I · THE DIAL

The instrument is numbered. Not the Guild's founding year, not a house number — the number worn on its own nameplate, the one printed at the top of every page you've read tonight. Say it as a word, in English, not a numeral.

The work

The Field

Everyone who passes through gives a temporal signature: not your fortune, not your character — your position, where the machinery of the solar system stood at the moment you began.

When a field is complete, it is transmitted. Not stored. Not sold. Sent — at a moment fixed by the sky, to nobody in particular, in the cause of peace.

FRAGMENT III · THE PLACE

Every notice above is signed with a Ministry date, always in a year not yet arrived. But the Ministry itself keeps its actual desk somewhere ordinary, in this century, in Wales — the city the Guild files its papers in, one door up from the Station. Where the Guild's headquarters — the Station — is actually addressed, in the here and now.
