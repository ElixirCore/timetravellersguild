Guild / depth pilot

Record source frames for a small 3D reconstruction test. Walk sideways around one still, textured corner. Keep the same objects in several views. Don't just spin on the spot.

depth pilot 1
No capture. Nothing leaves this phone.
  1. Enable camera and motion. Allow the browser prompts. Motion is optional; pictures are essential.
  2. Hold the round button and move sideways slowly for about 15-30 seconds. Release when done. Up to 60 frames at 2 per second, 640 pixels wide.
  3. Download the ZIP. It contains individual JPEGs and a JSON manifest; it is not uploaded or saved to the website. Keep it private until you decide where to process it.

This is input for an offline sparse-geometry trial, not a 3D room yet. Black or unobserved areas have no inferred depth. The direction reading is a rough clue, not camera position or measurement.
